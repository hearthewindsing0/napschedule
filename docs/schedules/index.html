Nap schedules by age

Babywise eat-wake-sleep schedules for the first year, then toddler nap schedules up to age 5.

Birth to 12 months: Babywise schedules

  • Babywise Weeks 1–2Naps: 5–6 · Feeds: 8–10 (at least 8)
  • Babywise Weeks 3–5Naps: 5–6 · Feeds: At least 8 (7–8 by week 5)
  • Babywise Weeks 6–7Naps: 4–6 · Feeds: 7–8
  • Babywise Weeks 8–12Naps: 4–5 · Feeds: 6–8 (the book lists at least 7 through week 10)
  • Babywise Weeks 13–15Naps: 3–4 · Feeds: About 5–6
  • Babywise Weeks 16–24 (4–5 months)Naps: 3–4 · Feeds: About 5
  • Babywise Weeks 25–38 (6–9 months)Naps: 2–3 · Feeds: About 4–5 milk feeds plus solids
  • Babywise Weeks 39–52 (9–12 months)Naps: 2 · Feeds: 3 meals, 1–2 snacks, milk

12 months to 5 years: toddler naps

  • 11–14 months2 naps · awake 3–4 hours
  • 15–18 months1–2 (usually 1) naps · awake 4.5–5.5 hours
  • 19–24 months1 naps · awake 5–6 hours
  • 2–3 years1 naps · awake 5–6 hours
  • 3–5 years0–1 naps · awake 5.5–6.5 hours (if napping)

This site shares general information, not medical advice. Every child is different. Talk with your pediatrician about your child's sleep, especially if you have concerns about health, growth, or breathing.
