11–14 months nap schedule

Nap schedule for 11 month to 14 month olds: 2 naps, 3–4 hour wake windows, the 12-month nap strike, and a sample day.

Total sleep (24 hrs)11–14 hours
Naps per day2
Wake windows3–4 hours
Daytime sleep2–3 hours
Typical bedtime7:00–8:00 PM

Around the first birthday, most babies still need two naps, even if they occasionally refuse one. Wake windows are about 3 to 4 hours and daytime sleep totals 2 to 3 hours.

Many parents see a "nap strike" around 12 months and assume it's time to drop to one nap. In most cases it's a temporary phase. Most children are better rested if they keep two naps until at least 13–15 months.

Following Babywise? The same predictable rhythm carries on into toddlerhood: consistent wake-up, meals, naps and bedtime at about the same times every day. The Ezzos cover this stage in On Becoming Pretoddlerwise and On Becoming Toddlerwise.

Tips for this age

  • If your toddler skips the morning nap, offer some quiet crib time anyway and move the afternoon nap earlier.
  • Shorten the morning nap (to about 45–60 minutes) to protect the afternoon nap.
  • Walking and early words can temporarily disrupt sleep. Stay consistent.
  • Bedtime can shift earlier (even to 6:30 PM) on days with only one nap.

What's next

When your toddler consistently (for 2+ weeks) resists one nap or takes a long time to fall asleep, it may be time to begin the 2-to-1 nap transition.

Frequently asked questions

Should my 1 year old take one nap or two?

Most 12 month olds still do best on two naps. The move to one nap usually happens between 13 and 18 months.

How long should a 1 year old nap?

Around 2 to 3 hours of total daytime sleep is typical.
