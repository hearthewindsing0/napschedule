15–18 months nap schedule

15 to 18 month nap schedule: transitioning to one midday nap, 5–5.5 hour wake windows, sample schedule, and tips.

Total sleep (24 hrs)11–14 hours
Naps per day1–2 (usually 1)
Wake windows4.5–5.5 hours
Daytime sleep2–3 hours
Typical bedtime7:00–8:00 PM

This is the age when most toddlers move from two naps to one. Once the switch is complete, a single midday nap of about 2 to 3 hours is typical, usually starting between 12:00 and 1:00 PM.

The transition itself can take a few weeks. Expect some overtired afternoons and occasional days where two naps are still needed. The sample schedule below shows a one-nap day.

Following Babywise? The same predictable rhythm carries on into toddlerhood: consistent wake-up, meals, naps and bedtime at about the same times every day. The Ezzos cover this stage in On Becoming Pretoddlerwise and On Becoming Toddlerwise.

Tips for this age

  • Move the morning nap later by 15–30 minutes every few days until it lands around midday.
  • Offer an earlier bedtime (by 30–60 minutes) during the transition to make up for lost sleep.
  • Keep a consistent lunch time just before nap; it makes a natural cue.
  • If your toddler goes to daycare, they may make the switch there first. Mirror the daycare schedule on weekends.

What's next

Once on one nap, most toddlers keep it until somewhere between ages 3 and 5.

Frequently asked questions

When do toddlers switch to one nap?

Most toddlers transition to one nap between 13 and 18 months, with 15 months being very common.

What time should an 18 month old nap?

A nap starting around 12:00 to 1:00 PM and lasting 1.5 to 3 hours works for most.
