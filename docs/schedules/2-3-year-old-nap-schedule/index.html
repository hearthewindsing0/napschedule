2–3 years nap schedule

Two and three year olds generally take one afternoon nap of about 1 to 2 hours and sleep 10 to 12 hours at night. The nap often starts around 12:30 to 1:00 PM.

Moving to a big-kid bed, potty training, or a new sibling can all shake up naps at this age. Keeping the nap on the calendar, even when it's sometimes refused, usually pays off in better evenings.

Following Babywise? The same predictable rhythm carries on into toddlerhood: consistent wake-up, meals, naps and bedtime at about the same times every day. The Ezzos cover this stage in On Becoming Pretoddlerwise and On Becoming Toddlerwise.

Tips for this age

  • Consider keeping your child in a crib until closer to age 3 if they're sleeping well there.
  • A toddler clock that changes color can help children understand when nap is over.
  • On days the nap is skipped, plan an earlier bedtime.
  • If bedtime becomes very late or a struggle, try shortening the nap before dropping it.

What's next

Many children start dropping the nap between 3 and 4, and most have stopped napping by 5. See when do kids stop napping.

Frequently asked questions

Do 3 year olds still need a nap?

Many do. Roughly half of children still nap at age 3. Watch your child's mood and night sleep rather than their age alone.

How long should a 2 year old's nap be?

About 1 to 2 hours. If naps are long and bedtime is late, try capping it at 1.5 hours.
