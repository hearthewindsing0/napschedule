6–7 months nap schedule

6 and 7 month old nap schedule with 3 naps, 2–2.5 hour wake windows, a sample day, and signs your baby is ready for 2 naps.

Total sleep (24 hrs)12–16 hours
Naps per day3 (moving to 2)
Wake windows2–2.5 hours
Daytime sleep3–3.5 hours
Typical bedtime7:00–8:00 PM

By six months, most babies settle into three naps a day: a morning nap, a longer midday nap, and a short late-afternoon catnap. Wake windows are usually around 2 to 2.5 hours, and daytime sleep totals about 3 to 3.5 hours.

This is also a busy developmental stretch. Sitting, rolling both ways, and starting solids can all temporarily disrupt naps. Keep the routine consistent and most bumps pass within a week or two.

Tips for this age

  • Keep the third nap short (20–45 minutes) so it doesn't push bedtime too late.
  • Offer plenty of practice time for new skills during awake periods, so your baby is less tempted to practice in the crib.
  • If naps suddenly shorten, try stretching the wake window before that nap by 15 minutes.
  • Solid foods don't need to be timed around naps yet; milk is still the main source of nutrition.

What's next

Most babies drop the third nap between 7 and 9 months. Signs include refusing the catnap, a later bedtime, or early-morning wakings. See our 3-to-2 nap transition guide.

Frequently asked questions

How many naps does a 6 month old need?

Most 6 month olds need three naps per day, totaling about 3 to 3.5 hours of daytime sleep.

When do babies go from 3 naps to 2?

Typically between 7 and 9 months, once they can comfortably stay awake for about 2.5 to 3 hours.
