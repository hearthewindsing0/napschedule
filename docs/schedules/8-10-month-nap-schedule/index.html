8–10 months nap schedule

Nap schedule for 8, 9 and 10 month old babies: 2 naps, 2.5–3.75 hour wake windows, bedtime tips, and a sample schedule.

Total sleep (24 hrs)12–16 hours
Naps per day2
Wake windows2.5–3.75 hours
Daytime sleep2.5–3 hours
Typical bedtime7:00–7:30 PM

Between eight and ten months, almost all babies are on a two-nap schedule: one in the late morning and one in the early-to-mid afternoon. Wake windows grow to roughly 2.5 to 3.75 hours, with the longest stretch before bedtime.

Crawling, pulling to stand, and a new awareness of separation can all show up at naptime. Some families notice an 8–10 month sleep regression; it's usually temporary when the schedule stays steady.

Tips for this age

  • The two-nap schedule is very stable, so this is a great time for a consistent daily rhythm.
  • If your baby stands up in the crib and can't get down, practice lowering to sitting during play.
  • Cap the second nap if bedtime is becoming a battle; aim for it to end about 3.5 hours before bed.
  • Separation anxiety is normal. A short, predictable goodbye routine at naptime helps.

What's next

Two naps usually last until somewhere between 13 and 18 months. Resist dropping to one nap too early; a short-lived nap strike around 12 months is common and usually passes.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a 9 month old nap?

About 2.5 to 3 hours of daytime sleep split across two naps is typical.

What time should a 9 month old go to bed?

Most do well with bedtime around 7:00 to 7:30 PM, about 3.5 hours after the second nap ends.

This site shares general information, not medical advice. Every child is different. Talk with your pediatrician about your child's sleep, especially if you have concerns about health, growth, or breathing.
