2–3 months nap schedule

Nap schedule for 2 and 3 month old babies: 4–5 naps, 60–90 minute wake windows, and a sample daily schedule.

Total sleep (24 hrs)14–17 hours
Naps per day4–5
Wake windows60–90 minutes
Daytime sleep4–5 hours
Typical bedtime8:00–10:00 PM, moving earlier

Between two and three months, babies become more alert and the day starts to take shape. Most take four or five naps and can stay awake for roughly 60 to 90 minutes at a time. Naps are still often short (30 to 45 minutes is very common), and that's expected while sleep cycles mature.

This is a great age to start a simple, repeatable nap routine: a diaper change, a darkened room, a sleep sack, and a short song. You're not aiming for a rigid clock yet, just a predictable pattern.

Tips for this age

  • Start the day at a consistent time (within about 30 minutes) to anchor the rest of the schedule.
  • Use wake windows rather than the clock: the first wake window of the day is usually the shortest.
  • A late-afternoon catnap of 20–30 minutes helps bridge the gap to bedtime.
  • Begin putting your baby down drowsy but awake for at least one nap a day, if that suits your family.
  • Bedtime tends to drift earlier toward the end of this stage as night sleep consolidates.

What's next

Around 4 months, sleep architecture changes (the so-called 4-month regression), wake windows stretch to 1.5–2 hours, and most babies move toward 3–4 naps.

Frequently asked questions

How many naps should a 3 month old take?

Most 3 month olds take 4 to 5 naps a day, adding up to around 4 to 5 hours of daytime sleep.

Why are my 2 month old's naps only 30 minutes?

One sleep cycle at this age is roughly 30–45 minutes, and many babies haven't learned to link cycles yet. Short naps usually lengthen between 4 and 6 months.
