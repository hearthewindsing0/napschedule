Babywise schedule: Weeks 3–5

A Babywise-style schedule for weeks 3 to 5: feeds every 2.5–3 hours, short waketimes, 1.5–2 hour naps and a sample day.

Naps per day5–6
Total sleep (24 hrs)16–18 hours
Feeds per dayAt least 8 (7–8 by week 5)
Time between feedsEvery 2.5–3 hours during the day
Waketime (our estimate)About 20 minutes or a bit more after the feed

Naps, total sleep and feed counts are from On Becoming Babywise (25th Anniversary Edition) by Gary Ezzo and Robert Bucknam, MD, summarized in our own words. Waketime is our estimate.

Between weeks 3 and 5, babies start to have a little real waketime after eating. In the Babywise model, feeding, burping and changing take about half an hour, followed by a short stretch awake, then a nap of roughly 1.5 to 2 hours before the next feed.

Daytime feeds usually land 2.5 to 3 hours apart. The book's goal is a predictable rhythm, with the first feed of the morning at the same time each day.

Tips for this stage

  • Pick a consistent first-feed time (for example, 7:00 AM) and wake your baby for it. That anchors the whole day.
  • Keep waketime calm: a few minutes on a play mat, looking at faces, a short walk.
  • If a nap ends early and your baby is genuinely hungry, feed them. Then return to the routine at the next cycle.
  • Keep checking diapers and weight gain with your pediatrician.

About Babywise and feeding schedules: The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends feeding babies when they show hunger cues, and in 1998 it raised concerns that strict schedule-based feeding can lead to poor weight gain, dehydration and low milk supply. If you use a Babywise-style routine, treat feeding times as flexible, feed your baby whenever they are hungry, and keep up with weight checks at your pediatrician.
