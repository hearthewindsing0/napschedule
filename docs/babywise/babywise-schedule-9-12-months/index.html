Babywise-style schedule · 9–12 months

Weeks 39–52 eat-wake-sleep schedule

Babywise-style schedule for 9 to 12 months: three meals plus snacks, 2 naps, and an 8 PM bedtime.

Feeds a day

About 2.5–3 hours

Naps, total sleep and feed counts are from On Becoming Babywise (25th Anniversary Edition) by Gary Ezzo and Robert Bucknam, MD, summarized in our own words. Waketime is our estimate.

Toward the first birthday, the Babywise routine looks a lot like a family schedule: breakfast, a morning nap, lunch, an afternoon nap, a snack, family dinner and an 8 PM bedtime. The book notes that by around 46 to 52 weeks, many babies no longer need a liquid feed before bed.

From here, the Ezzos' follow-up books (Babywise II, On Becoming Pretoddlerwise and On Becoming Toddlerwise) cover the move to one nap and toddler routines.

Tips for this stage

  • Keep naps in the crib at consistent times; two naps usually last until somewhere between 13 and 18 months.
  • Talk with your pediatrician about milk amounts and switching to whole milk around 12 months.

About Babywise and feeding schedules: The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends feeding babies when they show hunger cues, and in 1998 it raised concerns that strict schedule-based feeding can lead to poor weight gain, dehydration and low milk supply. If you use a Babywise-style routine, treat feeding times as flexible, feed your baby whenever they are hungry, and keep up with weight checks at your pediatrician.
