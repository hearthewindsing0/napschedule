Babywise schedule: Weeks 16–24 (4–5 months)

Babywise-style 4 and 5 month schedule: five feed-wake-sleep cycles, 3–4 naps with a catnap, and an 8 PM bedtime.

Naps per day3–4
Total sleep (24 hrs)13–16 hours
Feeds per dayAbout 5
Time between feedsAbout every 3–3.5 hours
Waketime (our estimate)About 1.5–2 hours including the feed

Naps, total sleep and feed counts are from On Becoming Babywise (25th Anniversary Edition) by Gary Ezzo and Robert Bucknam, MD, summarized in our own words. Waketime is our estimate.

By four to five months, Babywise describes a day of about five cycles: four daytime feeds and a liquid feed before bed around 8 PM. The late-evening feed is usually gone by this point.

Ask your pediatrician about starting solids; the AAP recommends introducing solid foods around 6 months, when your baby shows signs of readiness.

Tips for this stage

  • Keep the catnap short so bedtime stays on track.
  • If your baby starts waking at night again, check whether daytime feeds have gotten shorter or more distracted.

About Babywise and feeding schedules: The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends feeding babies when they show hunger cues, and in 1998 it raised concerns that strict schedule-based feeding can lead to poor weight gain, dehydration and low milk supply. If you use a Babywise-style routine, treat feeding times as flexible, feed your baby whenever they are hungry, and keep up with weight checks at your pediatrician.
