Babywise-style schedule · 2–3 months

Weeks 8–12 eat-wake-sleep schedule

A Babywise-style 2 to 3 month schedule: 3-hour feed-wake-sleep cycles, 4–5 naps, a late-evening feed and longer nights.

Naps a day
4–5
Total sleep
14–17 hours
Feeds a day
6–8 (the book lists at least 7 through week 10)
Between feeds
About every 3 hours
Waketime (our estimate)
About 60–90 minutes including the feed

Naps, total sleep and feed counts are from On Becoming Babywise (25th Anniversary Edition) by Gary Ezzo and Robert Bucknam, MD, summarized in our own words. Waketime is our estimate.

This is the stage most parents picture when they think of Babywise. The day falls into about five 3-hour cycles, plus a late-evening feed around 10:30 to 11 PM. Babywise says many babies who follow the routine sleep through the night somewhere around 8 to 12 weeks. Plenty of healthy babies take longer, and that's normal too.

Waketime is now long enough for real play: tummy time, songs, a walk outside.

Tips for this stage

  • If your baby still wakes at night hungry, feed them. A long night stretch comes from full daytime feeds, not from ignoring hunger.
  • Protect the late-evening feed for now; it's the one that usually carries babies through the night.
  • Bring your baby to their pediatrician visits and keep an eye on weight gain as nights lengthen.

About Babywise and feeding schedules: The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends feeding babies when they show hunger cues, and in 1998 it raised concerns that strict schedule-based feeding can lead to poor weight gain, dehydration and low milk supply. If you use a Babywise-style routine, treat feeding times as flexible, feed your baby whenever they are hungry, and keep up with weight checks at your pediatrician.
