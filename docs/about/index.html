About NapSchedule

NapSchedule.com is a simple, no-nonsense reference for baby and toddler nap schedules. Our goal is to give tired parents clear, practical answers: how many naps, how long between them, and what a typical day looks like at each age.

Our schedules are based on widely used pediatric sleep guidance, including the total sleep recommendations from the American Academy of Sleep Medicine (endorsed by the American Academy of Pediatrics) and the AAP's safe sleep recommendations. Typical ranges are just that: typical. Your child may need a little more or less.

We keep the site free with advertising and affiliate links. See our privacy policy and disclaimer.

This site shares general information, not medical advice. Every child is different. Talk with your pediatrician about your child's sleep, especially if you have concerns about health, growth, or breathing.
