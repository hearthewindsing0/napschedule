About NapSchedule

NapSchedule.com is a simple, no-nonsense reference for baby and toddler nap schedules. Our goal is to give tired parents clear, practical answers: how many naps, how long between them, and what a typical day looks like at each age.

Our first-year schedules follow the eat-wake-sleep routine described in On Becoming Babywise (25th Anniversary Edition) by Gary Ezzo and Robert Bucknam, MD, summarized in our own words. Toddler schedules use the total sleep recommendations from the American Academy of Sleep Medicine (endorsed by the American Academy of Pediatrics), and our safety advice follows the AAP's safe sleep recommendations. We are not affiliated with the Babywise authors or publisher.

Because the AAP recommends feeding babies on hunger cues, we always present Babywise feed times as a flexible guide. Your baby's hunger and growth come first.

We keep the site free with advertising and affiliate links. See our privacy policy and disclaimer.

This site shares general information, not medical advice. Every child is different. Talk with your pediatrician about your child's sleep, especially if you have concerns about health, growth, or breathing.
