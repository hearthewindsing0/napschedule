Moving from 3 naps to 2

When to drop the third nap (usually 7–9 months), signs your baby is ready, and how to make the 3 to 2 nap transition smoothly.

Most babies move from three naps to two between 7 and 9 months. The nap that goes is the short late-afternoon catnap.

Signs your baby is ready

  • They consistently refuse or fight the third nap for a week or more.
  • Taking the third nap pushes bedtime later than you'd like (past about 8 PM).
  • They can stay awake around 3 hours without falling apart.
  • Night sleep or early mornings are getting worse even though nothing else has changed.

How to make the switch

  1. Stretch wake windows gradually, by 10–15 minutes every few days, so the first two naps move later.
  2. Let the second nap end around 3:00–3:30 PM, then aim for bedtime about 3.5 hours later.
  3. Use an earlier bedtime (even 6:30 PM) during the transition on days when naps are short.
  4. Keep a third catnap in your back pocket for days with very short naps. A 15–20 minute stroller or car nap is fine.

Frequently asked questions

What age do babies drop to 2 naps?

Most babies drop from three naps to two between 7 and 9 months.

Should I drop the 3rd nap if it's short?

A short third nap is normal and expected. Drop it when your baby regularly refuses it or when it makes bedtime too late.
