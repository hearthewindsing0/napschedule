Frequently asked questions

What is a Babywise schedule?

A Babywise schedule follows the routine from On Becoming Babywise: the day repeats in feed, waketime, sleep cycles, with feeds about 2.5 to 3 hours apart in the early weeks and fewer, fuller feeds as the baby grows.

How many naps does a Babywise baby take?

According to the book, about 5 to 6 naps in the first weeks, 4 to 5 around 2 to 3 months, 3 to 4 from about 3 to 6 months, 2 to 3 from 6 to 9 months, and 2 by 9 to 12 months.

When do toddlers drop to one nap?

Most toddlers move from two naps to one between 13 and 18 months.

About Babywise and feeding schedules: The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends feeding babies when they show hunger cues, and in 1998 it raised concerns that strict schedule-based feeding can lead to poor weight gain, dehydration and low milk supply. If you use a Babywise-style routine, treat feeding times as flexible, feed your baby whenever they are hungry, and keep up with weight checks at your pediatrician.
