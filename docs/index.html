Frequently asked questions

How many naps does a baby need?

It depends on age: newborns take 4–7 naps, 4–5 month olds take 3–4, 6–7 month olds take 3, babies from about 8 months to 15 months take 2, and most toddlers take 1 nap until somewhere between ages 3 and 5.

When do babies drop to one nap?

Most toddlers move from two naps to one between 13 and 18 months.

What is a wake window?

A wake window is the time a child is awake between sleeps. It grows from under an hour for newborns to 5–6 hours for toddlers.

This site shares general information, not medical advice. Every child is different. Talk with your pediatrician about your child's sleep, especially if you have concerns about health, growth, or breathing.
