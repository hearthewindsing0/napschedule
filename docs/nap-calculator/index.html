Nap time calculator

Enter your child's age and what time they woke up. We'll suggest nap times and a bedtime based on typical wake windows.

How to use these times

The calculator assumes each nap lasts a typical length for your child's age. Real naps vary, so treat the result as a plan for the day, not a rule. If a nap ends early, the next one usually needs to start earlier. For newborns, follow your baby's hunger and sleepy cues first.

Frequently asked questions

How does the nap calculator work?

It uses typical wake windows and nap lengths for your child's age, starting from the time they woke up, to estimate when each nap and bedtime should fall.

What if my baby's nap runs shorter or longer?

Use the times as a starting point. If a nap is shorter than planned, the next nap usually comes a little earlier; if it's longer, a little later. Watch for sleepy cues.

This site shares general information, not medical advice. Every child is different. Talk with your pediatrician about your child's sleep, especially if you have concerns about health, growth, or breathing.
